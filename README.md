"I’m building GGai, an open-source AI unit designed to move beyond niche tasks and handle general-purpose inquiry. While many AI tools are locked behind complex APIs or rigid frameworks, this project focuses on creating a flexible, local-first assistant that can plug directly into your existing workflow.
It’s currently a work-in-progress, and I’m actively looking for early testers and contributors to help refine its reasoning capabilities and expand its knowledge base. If you’re interested in the future of accessible, general-purpose AI, check out the roadmap on GitHub!"
